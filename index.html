Applied ML Research

Domain-Specialized SLM Fine-Tuning — Llama 3.2 3B QLoRA for US Community-Healthcare Q&A

Independent Project  ·  2025

Built an end-to-end, fully local pipeline to download, ingest, and process ~3,200 publicly-available Community Health Needs Assessment (CHNA) reports from US hospitals across all 50 states and territories, then fine-tuned a Llama 3.2 3B Instruct base into a CHNA-domain Q&A specialist answering complex questions about healthcare infrastructure, resource gaps, and demographic needs. No proprietary APIs in data prep, training, or evaluation.

  • Training architecture. LoRA rank 32 across attention + MLP; 4-bit NF4 + double-quant; paged AdamW 8-bit; gradient checkpointing; NEFTune embedding noise; single consumer GPU (RTX 4090, 24 GB). Synthetic Q&A generated by a local Ollama teacher (Llama 3.1 8B Q4) with faithfulness filtering.
  • Hospital-level holdout splits. Train / val / test partitioned by hospital, not by row — a hospital appearing in train cannot leak into eval, preventing the model from memorizing near-identical annual CHNAs.
  • Multi-metric evaluation. Perplexity, pairwise judging vs base (Qwen 2.5 7B as the LLM-as-judge, different family from teacher to reduce shared bias), 1–5 rubric, ROUGE-L, and faithfulness on the held-out test set. Pairwise win-rate vs base Llama 3.2 3B Instruct = 68% (n=300); rubric avg = 4.0 / 5.0 (n=500); faithfulness = 0.78.
